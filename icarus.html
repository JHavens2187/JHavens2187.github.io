Ingestion & Two-Pass Global Continuum Cascade

ICARUS standardizes heterogeneous survey spectra through an immutable Spectrum1D container and built-in instrument presets (e.g., jwst_nirspec_medium). Before fitting emission lines across 22 rest-frame UV/optical/NIR windows, Stage 1 isolates the underlying AGN and host-galaxy continua.

Dynamic $O(1)$ Normalization

Computes a dynamic scale factor per spectrum, standardizing flux arrays to $O(1)$ space via $10^{\lfloor\log_{10}(\text{median flux})\rfloor}$. This prevents optimizer underflow on faint high-$z$ targets ($\sim 10^{-20}\ \text{erg s}^{-1}\ \text{cm}^{-2}\ \text{\AA}^{-1}$) before restoring true physical units.

2-Pass Continuum & Fe II

Pass 1 fits the global AGN broken power-law and Balmer recombination continuum ($3646\ \text{\AA}$ edge). Pass 2 models broadened UV and optical $\text{Fe\,\textsc{ii}}$ pseudo-continua, anchored locally by outer-25% window medians.

Cosmological Projection

Relying on an underlying Planck18 / FlatLambdaCDM cosmology, raw observational vectors are cleanly projected into rest-frame coordinates and deconvolved against wavelength-dependent instrumental LSF curves.

The Kinematic Decision Engine

Stage 2 evaluates blended multi-component emission complexes to decouple Broad-Line Regions (BLR), biconical $[\text{O\,\textsc{iii}}]$ outflows, and gravitational narrow cores without manual intervention.

  • $\Delta\text{BIC} \leq -10$ Arbitration: Nested models (Narrow-Only vs. $[\text{O\,\textsc{iii}}]$ Outflow vs. Broad BLR) must survive a strict Bayesian Information Criterion threshold and physical peak/width vetoes to eliminate false positives.
  • Quantum Doublet Tying: Algebraic constraints lock $[\text{O\,\textsc{iii}}]\ \lambda5007/\lambda4959$ to $2.98:1$ and $[\text{N\,\textsc{ii}}]\ \lambda6583/\lambda6548$ to $2.95:1$, paired with coupled systemic narrow-line velocity offsets ($\Delta v_{\text{narrow}}$).
  • $2\sigma$ Speed Gatekeeper: Pre-screens weak or absent spectral windows before launching multi-component Levenberg-Marquardt optimization, cutting redundant evaluations while preserving $3\sigma$ upper limits.

Publication Diagnostics & Virial Waterfall

ICARUS generates a 5-panel diagnostic summary per target: full-range log-wavelength SED decomposition, zoomed $[\text{O\,\textsc{iii}}]+\text{H}\beta$ and $\text{H}\alpha+[\text{N\,\textsc{ii}}]$ velocity panels with $\pm 3\sigma$ pull strips, a BPT nebular excitation diagram, and an Executive Telemetry Card.

Automated Astrophysical Yields:

Upon convergence, an automated Virial Waterfall deconvolves instrumental broadening to derive single-epoch Black Hole Masses ($\log_{10}(M_{\text{BH}}/M_\odot)$ via $\text{H}\beta$, $\text{H}\alpha$, $\text{Mg\,\textsc{ii}}$, or $\text{Pa}\beta$), Bolometric Luminosities ($\log L_{\text{bol}}$), Eddington Ratios ($\lambda_{\text{Edd}}$), narrow-line Star Formation Rates, and local RMS $3\sigma$ upper limits.

Blind Radiative-Transfer Validation & KU HPC Scaling

To prevent circular ($A=A$) testing, ICARUS v3.4 was validated against an independent Cloudy C23 synthetic suite featuring broken accretion disks, host $\text{D}_n4000$ breaks with stellar Balmer absorption, and non-Gaussian Voigt BLR profiles on KU High-Performance Computing (HPC) SLURM nodes.

The Middleware Debugging Engine

The @dev_hook architectural pattern embeds a non-destructive event emitter and Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) registry throughout the pipeline. This allows developers to pass a .dev telemetry configuration file to inject synthetic data, force algorithmic failures, trace memory usage, or actively bypass standard multiprocessing for interactive terminal shell pauses—all without altering production code.
